How It Works

The Spectrum Matrix

Each story is visualized on a 3×3 matrix showing how different sources cover it. Source logos are plotted based on their political leaning and tone.

Reading the Matrix

Horizontal axis (Political): Left-leaning sources on the left, centrist in the middle, right-leaning on the right.

Vertical axis (Tone): Optimistic coverage at the top, neutral in the middle, pessimistic at the bottom.
